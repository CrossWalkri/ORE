# STRUCK

This specification now lives in its own repository as **STRUCK**, the exit-boundary standard:

**https://github.com/CrossWalkri/STRUCK**

STRUCK specifies what an evidence-grade output owes on its face before anyone acts on it: **S**upport graded, **T**raced to origin, **R**efutation stated, **U**njudged worth, **C**ontest kept, **K**nown on its face. The five obligations and the conformance principle are unchanged from what was published at this path; only the name and location changed (Naming Decision 2.57). CC0 1.0.
